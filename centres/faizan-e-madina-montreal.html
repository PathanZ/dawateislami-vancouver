About this centre

Faizan-e-Madina Montreal

Faizan-e-Madina Montreal is a Dawat-e-Islami centre serving the Muslim community of Parc-Extension, Montreal. It is a place for prayer, Islamic learning and spiritual gatherings, open to everyone.

Like every Dawat-e-Islami centre, it holds a weekly spiritual gathering and offers Qur'an education for children and adults. Contact the centre for its current timings.
