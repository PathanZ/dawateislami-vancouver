About the Jamia

Free scholarly training

Jamia-tul-Madina is Dawat-e-Islami's network of Islamic universities, with more than 1,500 branches worldwide. Students study the traditional Dars-e-Nizami (Aalim) course: Qur'an, Hadith, Islamic law, Arabic and the other Islamic sciences.

In Canada, the Jamia runs a full-time Aalim course in Montreal, free of charge for accepted students, and is opening a new permanent campus in Cornwall, Ontario.
