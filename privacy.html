Privacy policy

How this website handles your information

Last updated: October 2026. Draft, to be reviewed by management

What we collect

This website does not ask you to create an account and does not use advertising or tracking cookies. We may use privacy-friendly visitor statistics (such as Cloudflare Web Analytics) that count page visits without identifying you.

When you contact us

If you email or phone us, we use your details only to reply to you, and we do not sell or share them.

Donations

Online donations are processed by a third-party payment provider, and Interac e-Transfers by your bank. Their privacy policies apply. We keep the name and address you give us only to issue tax receipts and to meet the Canada Revenue Agency's record-keeping requirements.

Links to other websites

This site links to other Dawat-e-Islami websites, YouTube and map services, which have their own privacy policies.

Questions

For any privacy question, email canada@dawateislami.net.
