Departments

The departments that carry out the work of Dawat-e-Islami Canada

Each department focuses on one area of service, from Qur'an education to welfare and media.

Madrasa-tul-Madina for Boys

Free Qur'an reading, tajweed and Hifz classes for boys.

Madrasa-tul-Madina for Girls

Free Qur'an reading, tajweed and Hifz classes for girls, taught by female teachers.

Madrasa-tul-Madina for Adults

Qur'an classes for adult brothers and sisters, at any level.

Faizan Weekly Islamic School

Weekend Islamic education for children in public school.

Jamia-tul-Madina

Training future Islamic scholars, in Montreal, Quebec.

Islamic Courses

Short courses on the essentials of Islamic knowledge and practice.

Dar-ul-Ifta Ahl-e-Sunnat

Answers to religious questions from qualified muftis.

Spiritual Cures

Comfort and guidance through Qur'anic and prophetic remedies.

Madani Qafila

Groups that travel to learn and share the teachings of Islam.

Khuddam-ul-Masajid

Building, maintaining and serving our masajid and centres.

FGRF

Faizan Global Relief Foundation, Dawat-e-Islami's welfare and relief work.

Health Care

Support and guidance for the community's health and wellbeing.

Madani Channel

Dawat-e-Islami's Islamic television channel, in English and other languages.

Donation Boxes

Collection boxes that support the work of Dawat-e-Islami.

Finances

Managing donations transparently and responsibly.

I.T.

Websites, apps and technology for Dawat-e-Islami Canada.

A global, non-political movement spreading the teachings of the Qur'an and Sunnah, with 13 centres across Canada.
