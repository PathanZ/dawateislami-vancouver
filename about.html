Who we are

Dawat-e-Islami

Dawat-e-Islami is a global, non-political Islamic organization working to spread the teachings of the Qur'an and Sunnah. It was founded in 1981 in Karachi, Pakistan, by Ameer-e-Ahl-e-Sunnat, Hazrat Allama Maulana Muhammad Ilyas Attar Qadri Razavi.

Today its departments serve communities in many countries: masajid and Islamic centres, Qur'an classes, Islamic schools, Jamias that train scholars, spiritual cure, funeral services, the Madani Channel and welfare work.

Its work rests on four values: the Qur'an and Sunnah, education, welfare and spiritual reform.

In Canada

Dawat-e-Islami Canada

Dawat-e-Islami Canada carries this work into Canadian communities, with 13 centres in 6 provinces and Jamia-tul-Madina campuses in Montreal and Cornwall.

  • Weekly gatherings for brothers and sisters
  • Friday prayers and daily congregational prayers
  • Qur'an classes for children and adults
  • Weekend Islamic school and youth classes
  • Scholarly training at Jamia-tul-Madina
