About Dawat-e-Islami

✦

Dawat-e-Islami is an international, non-political Islamic organization working to spread the message of the Qur'an and Sunnah across the world. It was established in 1981 in Karachi, Pakistan, by one of the great scholars of the present era, Ameer-e-Ahl-e-Sunnat, Allama Maulana Abu Bilal Muhammad Ilyas Attar Qadri.

Today Dawat-e-Islami serves communities in many countries through Qur'an education, Islamic scholarship, publishing, the Madani Channel and welfare work.

Our founder

Ameer-e-Ahl-e-Sunnat

✦

Shaykh-e-Tariqat, Ameer-e-Ahl-e-Sunnat, Allama Maulana Abu Bilal Muhammad Ilyas Attar Qadri Razavi is the founder of Dawat-e-Islami. Through his books, bayans and the Madani Muzakra, he has inspired people around the world to live by the Qur'an and Sunnah.

In Canada

Dawat-e-Islami Canada

✦

Dawat-e-Islami has 13 centres across Canada, in British Columbia, Alberta, Saskatchewan, Manitoba, Ontario and Quebec. Through Jamia-tul-Madina in Montreal, Quebec, it is also producing the next generation of scholars.

  • Weekly Islamic gatherings for brothers and sisters
  • Zikr sessions
  • Qur'an classes for adults and children
  • Friday prayers
  • Youth gatherings in provinces, cities and local areas
