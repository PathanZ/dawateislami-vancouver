About us

Dawat-e-Islami Canada

Dawat-e-Islami is a global, non-political Islamic organization working to spread the Qur'an and Sunnah. It was founded in 1981 in Karachi by Ameer-e-Ahl-e-Sunnat, Allama Maulana Muhammad Ilyas Attar Qadri.

Its departments run masajid, Qur'an classes, Islamic schools, Jamias, spiritual cure and welfare work. In Canada, this work reaches communities in 6 provinces.

Discover Islam

The five pillars of Islam

شَهَادَةFirst pillar

Shahadah

Bearing witness that there is none worthy of worship except Allah, and that Muhammad ﷺ is His Messenger.

 صَلَاةSecond pillar

Salah

Worshipping Allah through the five daily prayers.

 زَكَاةThird pillar

Zakat

Giving a portion of eligible wealth each year to those entitled to it.

 صَوْمFourth pillar

Sawm

Fasting from dawn to sunset through the month of Ramadan.

 حَجّFifth pillar

Hajj

Pilgrimage to Makkah once in a lifetime, for those who are able.
