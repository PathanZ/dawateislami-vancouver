About us

Dawat-e-Islami Canada

✦

Dawat-e-Islami is an international, non-political Islamic organization working to spread the message of the Qur'an and Sunnah. It was established in 1981 in Karachi, Pakistan, by Ameer-e-Ahl-e-Sunnat, Allama Maulana Abu Bilal Muhammad Ilyas Attar Qadri.

In Canada, Dawat-e-Islami has 13 centres across 6 provinces, offering weekly gatherings, Qur'an classes for adults and children, Friday prayers and youth programs.

Discover Islam

The five pillars of Islam

✦
شَهَادَة

Shahadah

Belief in the oneness of Allah and that Muhammad ﷺ is His final messenger.

صَلَاة

Salah

Worshipping Allah through the five daily prayers.

زَكَاة

Zakat

Giving 2.5% of eligible wealth each year to those in need.

صَوْم

Sawm

Fasting from dawn to sunset through the month of Ramadan.

حَجّ

Hajj

Pilgrimage to Makkah once in a lifetime, for those who are able.
