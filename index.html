    About us

    Part of the global family of Dawat-e-Islami

    ✦

    Dawat-e-Islami was founded in 1981 in Karachi by Ameer-e-Ahl-e-Sunnat, Maulana Muhammad Ilyas Attar Qadri. Today it serves communities around the world through Qur'an education, Islamic scholarship and welfare work.

    Dawat-e-Islami Canada brings that work to communities across the country. Its work is guided by the Madani Maqsad: to strive to reform ourselves and the people of the entire world.

    Our services to the community

    What we offer

    ✦

    At Faizan e Madina, Surrey. All programs are open to the community.

    5 daily prayers

    Jama'at for all five daily prayers at Faizan e Madina, Surrey.

    Two Jummah prayers

    Two Friday congregations, at 1:30 PM and 3:30 PM.

    Weekly Ijtima

    Every Thursday at 7:00 PM: tilawat, naat, bayan, zikr, dua and salam.

    Weekend Islamic School

    Faith, character and Qur'an for children who attend school during the week.

    Free monthly courses

    Short courses on everyday Islamic knowledge, open to everyone.

    Qur'an classes & Hifz

    Learn to read the Qur'an with tajweed, and memorise it in the Hifz program.

    Sisters-only Halaqah

    Classes and gatherings run by and for Islamic sisters.

    Faizan Online Academy

    Live online Qur'an and Islamic studies classes you can join from home.

    Discover Islam

    The five pillars of Islam

    ✦
    شَهَادَة

    Shahadah

    Belief in the oneness of Allah and that Muhammad ﷺ is His final messenger.

    صَلَاة

    Salah

    Worshipping Allah through the five daily prayers.

    زَكَاة

    Zakat

    Giving 2.5% of eligible wealth each year to those in need.

    صَوْم

    Sawm

    Fasting from dawn to sunset through the month of Ramadan.

    حَجّ

    Hajj

    Pilgrimage to Makkah once in a lifetime, for those who are able.
