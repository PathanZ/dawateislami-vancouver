    About us

    Part of the global family of Dawat-e-Islami

    ✦

    Dawat-e-Islami was founded in 1981 in Karachi by Ameer-e-Ahl-e-Sunnat, Maulana Muhammad Ilyas Attar Qadri. Today it serves communities in countries around the world through Qur'an education, Islamic scholarship and welfare work.

    Faizan-e-Madina Surrey brings that work to our local community. Our mission follows the movement's motto: "I must strive to reform myself and the people of the entire world."

    Discover Islam

    The five pillars of Islam

    ✦
    شَهَادَة

    Shahadah

    Belief in the oneness of Allah and that Muhammad ﷺ is His final messenger.

    صَلَاة

    Salah

    Worshipping Allah through the five daily prayers.

    زَكَاة

    Zakat

    Giving 2.5% of eligible wealth each year to those in need.

    صَوْم

    Sawm

    Fasting from dawn to sunset through the month of Ramadan.

    حَجّ

    Hajj

    Pilgrimage to Makkah once in a lifetime, for those who are able.
