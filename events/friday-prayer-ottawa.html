Jumu'ah, Ottawa

Every Friday · 1:30 PM and 2:30 PM

Get directionsAbout the centre

Friday prayer

What to expect

Two Jumu'ah congregations: 1:30 PM and 2:30 PM (summer schedule). The speech begins 30 minutes before each Jama'at.

Everyone is welcome. Timings can change with the seasons, so please confirm with the centre before you travel.
