Sunnah-Inspired Ijtima, Calgary

Every Thursday · 7:30 PM

Get directionsAbout the centre

Weekly Ijtima

What to expect

A weekly gathering with recitation of the Qur'an, naat, a talk on faith and community, collective zikr and a closing dua.

Everyone is welcome. Timings can change with the seasons, so please confirm with the centre before you travel.
