Where your donation goes

Ways to give

General donation

For the general work of Dawat-e-Islami Canada and its centres.

Zakat

The obligatory annual charity, used only for those entitled to receive it.

Sadaqah

Voluntary charity of any amount, given at any time.

Fitrana

Sadaqat-ul-Fitr, paid before the Eid-ul-Fitr prayer for each member of the household.

Fidya

Compensation for fasts that cannot be kept because of illness or old age.

Build a masjid

Helps build, buy and pay the mortgage of our masajid and centres.

Sponsor a teacher

Supports a Qur'an teacher at one of our centres.

Jamia-tul-Madina

Supports the training of Islamic scholars in Canada.

Madrasa-tul-Madina

Supports free Qur'an classes for children and adults.

Madani Channel

Supports 24/7 Islamic television around the world.

Spiritual Cure

Supports free spiritual remedies for those in difficulty.

A global, non-political movement spreading the teachings of the Qur'an and Sunnah, with 13 centres across Canada.
