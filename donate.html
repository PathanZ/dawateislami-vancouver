Every donation helps keep our centres open, pays for Qur'an classes and supports the community. Please choose the type of donation so it is used correctly.

"Charity does not decrease wealth." Sahih Muslim, 2588

  • ZakatThe obligatory annual charity of 2.5% on eligible wealth, used only for those entitled to receive it.
  • SadaqahVoluntary charity of any amount, given at any time, for the general work of the centres.
  • FitrahSadaqat-ul-Fitr, paid before the Eid-ul-Fitr prayer on behalf of each member of the household.
  • Masjid and mortgageHelps pay the mortgage and running costs of our masajid and Islamic centres.
  • Qarz-e-HasanaAn interest-free loan to the centre, returned to you as agreed.
